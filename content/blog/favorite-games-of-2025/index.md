According to [the Steam Replay](https://s.team/y25/gvpbjbg?l=english) I played 78 games last year. In reality I probably played a few more than that but that's a good enough number. Here are my top games that I played last year in no particiular order

## Kingdom Come: Deliverence 2

![Kingdom Come: Deliverence 2](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1771300/header.jpg?t=1768306015)

I haven't beaten this one yet but I'm loving it. It's the ultimate medieval life simulator.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/1771300/Kingdom_Come_Deliverance_II/

## The Root Trees are Dead

![The Root Trees are Dead](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2754380/db66c1c1529ec8912ded4929282155cf05624a01/header.jpg?t=1768938325)

If you loved Obra Dinn do yourself a favor, stop what you are doing and go play this. It's one of the best "detective" games out there and my favorite of 2025.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/2754380/The_Roottrees_are_Dead/

## The Hundred Line: Last Defence Academy

![The Hundred Line: Last Defence Academy](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/3014080/header.jpg?t=1763608121)

After 140 hours I can say this game is pretty good. A wild and winding story that plays like 2 or 3 different games all wrapped up in 1 package.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/3014080/The_Hundred_Line_Last_Defense_Academy/

## PowerWash Simulator 2

![Powerwash Simulator 2](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2968420/91fea40a711c5a74cce1bb1a9e05c48a379f6aa0/header.jpg?t=1768317251)

This one is pure co-op fun. I played the whole game with my wife and loved every moment of it. The perfect game to space out to after a hard day.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/2968420/PowerWash_Simulator_2/

## Old School RuneScape

![Old Schol RuneScape](https://www.runescape.com/img/rsp777/title2/rslogo3.png)

Sailing came out in 2025 for OSRS. What can I say, I like to watch the numbers go up.

https://oldschool.runescape.com/

## In Stars and Time

![In stars and time](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1677310/header.jpg?t=1754400017)

This didn't come out in 2025 but I finally got around to playing it. This is the game that will stick most with me from 2025. A deeply heartfelt game about sadness.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/1677310/In_Stars_And_Time/

## Dungeons of Hinterberg

![Dungeons of Hinterberg](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1983260/header.jpg?t=1768497527)

A really fun combo of Persona life sim and hack 'n slash dungeons.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/1983260/Dungeons_of_Hinterberg/

## Cabernet

![Cabernet](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2401410/2f1fa18b4f4f5ac0cf508581ca34671216686e91/header.jpg?t=1766456617)

This was one of the best worlds in a game in 2025. Every moment of this game felt meaningful and like I was really effecting what was going to happen.

https://store.steampowered.com/app/2401410/Cabernet/

# Other Media

## Book of the Year: Tom's Crossing

From the House of Leaves author, this book is a fun westernish paranormalish story that blurs the line between reality and fiction.

https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/588857/toms-crossing-by-mark-z-danielewski/

## Managa of the Year: Akane-banashi

Learing about Rakugo after reading Akane-banashi has been a blast this year.

https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/chapters/akane-banashi

## Song of the Year: KAJ - Bara Bada Bastu

My wife and I fell in love with Eurovision last year. Which means we also fell in love with KAJ. I highly recommend looking up their musicals.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WK3HOMhAeQY

It's been 10 plus years since I discovered the game making community in Indiana.

In that time I've seen it ebb and flow.

Shrink and grow.

But its always there.

Game devs are Indiana's best kept secret.

Its hard to describe the emotion I get when I see the growth.

And In the past few years I've seen it grown so much.

I've seen it become so strong.

I'm glad I could see all the talent, creativity, and heart in the community.

I just hope it continues to grow.

I hope it keeps growing, making, and creating a place for others to grow.

And my ultimate hope is that I get to see the results of that growth.

Not just the games but the people who make the games.

Who make the community.

Who make it all worth it.

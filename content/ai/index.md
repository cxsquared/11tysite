# My Artificial Intelligence Policy

I don't use any LLMs or generative AI of any kind in my personal work. For survival I have used LLMs in my professional work as it was do that or get fired (spoilers, I got fired). For that usage I am deeply ashamed.

I maintain a human.json file to attest to my own humanity, and to vouch for the humanity of some of my friends’s websites. You can learn more about how human.json and the related browser extensions work in the Codeberg project.
